From India to Canada, a journey of passion

Avya means the first rays of the sun — the light that starts every new day.

Avya Jewels is rooted in more than ten years of experience in gold and silver jewellery. Our founder’s journey began in India with the support of her father, where she developed a deep appreciation for craftsmanship and for the memories each piece can hold.

Today she is building a new chapter in Canada alongside her husband, bringing that experience and passion to Avya Jewels. As a family venture, we believe jewellery is more than a luxury — it is a celebration of love, milestones and moments to cherish.

Every piece we choose is premium, elegant and affordable: 925 sterling silver jewellery, gold jewellery, and 99.99% pure gold and silver bars and coins for saving, investing and gifting.
