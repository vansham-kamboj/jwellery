Delivery, payment & returns

How ordering, paying and delivery work at Avya Jewels.

Delivery & pick-up

Free home delivery across the Greater Toronto Area on orders over $200, usually within 3–5 days.

Pick up from us in Mississauga, Ontario during opening hours (Monday to Sunday, 11 am to 7 pm).

We don’t ship outside the GTA yet — it’s in our plans.

Payment

Pay in full by Interac e-Transfer. After you place an order, we confirm it on WhatsApp within 15–30 minutes with payment details.

Listed prices include all taxes. Investment-grade gold and silver bullion carries no sales tax.

Gold & silver bullion

Bar and coin prices follow the live market, so we share today’s price on WhatsApp before you order.

Bars and coins come sealed, with their stated weight and purity and an assay certificate where applicable.

Returns & exchanges

All sales are final — we don’t accept returns or exchanges.

If you experience any issue with your purchase, please reach out. We’re happy to help, and we’ll do our best with any concern.

Care

Care instructions are included with every jewellery purchase. Keep silver in its pouch, wipe it with a soft cloth, and keep it away from perfume, chlorine and salt water.
